### About me

I'm  Sr enterprise  systems engineer (IT) with **10+ years of experience** working across collaboration platforms, identity and access management, SaaS integrations, and IT operations.

My focus is on building **secure foundations for automation and AI**: reliable authentication and provisioning, clear service ownership, sensible governance, and workflows that reduce manual work without sacrificing control or auditability.

I work at the intersection of **hands-on engineering, platform architecture, and service reliability**—from Slack Enterprise Grid and Okta to Google Workspace, n8n, and AI-enabled enterprise tools.

### Engineering principles

> **Foundation first.** Identity, access controls, ownership, observability, and recovery need to be designed into a system—not added after automation is deployed.

I favor practical designs with testable behavior, clear failure paths, and documentation that another engineer can operate.
